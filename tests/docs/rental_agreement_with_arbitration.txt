RENTAL AGREEMENT

This rental agreement is made on 1 January 2025 between Mr Rakesh Sharma (Landlord) and Ms Neha Verma (Tenant) for the flat at 12 Rose Villa, Kothrud, Pune.

1. TERM
The tenancy is for a period of eleven months commencing from 1 January 2025 and ending on 30 November 2025.

2. RENT AND DEPOSIT
The tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 20,000. The tenant has paid a security deposit of Rs. 60,000 to the landlord.

3. DEDUCTIONS
The landlord may deduct the cost of painting, not exceeding Rs. 5,000, from the security deposit. Any other deduction must be supported by a bill.

4. REFUND OF DEPOSIT
The landlord shall refund the security deposit within 15 days of the tenant vacating the premises.

5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this agreement shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Landlord. The decision of the arbitrator shall be final and binding on both parties.

6. NOTICES
Any notice under this agreement shall be given in writing to the address mentioned above.

Signed by the Landlord and the Tenant on 1 January 2025.
